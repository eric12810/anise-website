The story behind the dishes

Familiar flavours.
A fresh point of view.

Good food has a way of bringing people together. At Anise, we love the dishes we grew up with and the memories made around the table. Our creations begin there: with familiar flavours, thoughtful cooking and a curiosity for what might come next.

From a playful twist on a favourite to something entirely its own, each dish is an invitation to discover a new combination. Some are comforting, some are unexpected, and all are made to be enjoyed together.

Explore a few favourites from our kitchen, then find the one you would like to bring to yours.

A taste of Anise.

Seven creations, each with its own story. Made for sharing, discovering and coming back to.

Crispy Aromatic Duck Fried Rice

Crispy aromatic duck meets fried rice in our take on two familiar favourites. Enjoy a little of both in every spoonful.

Coconut Prawns

Golden coconut prawns bring a little something different to the table. Dip into the accompanying sauce and enjoy them between bites of your favourite dishes.

Gin Infused Lemon Chicken

A touch of gin gives familiar lemon chicken a playful twist. Bright lemon flavour makes this a lively addition to your meal.

Treasured Capital Spare Ribs

Our Treasured Capital Spare Ribs are a tempting way to begin a meal together. Set them in the centre of the table and let everyone tuck in.

Smoky Chicken Fried Rice

Smoky chicken fried rice is finished with a scattering of black sesame seeds. Enjoy it on its own or alongside a few favourites from the collection.

Sweet & Sour Meatballs

Glazed meatballs meet pineapple and colourful peppers in this sweet and sour favourite. Spoon them over rice to make the most of the sauce.

Prawns with Exotic Mushroom

Prawns and exotic mushrooms come together in a dish inspired by the joy of discovery. Add it to your usual order for a fresh combination to explore.

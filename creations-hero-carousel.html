The story behind the dishes

Familiar flavours.
A fresh point of view.

Good food has a way of bringing people together. At Anise, we love the dishes we grew up with and the memories made around the table. Our creations begin there: with familiar flavours, thoughtful cooking and a curiosity for what might come next.

From a playful twist on a favourite to something entirely its own, each dish is an invitation to discover a new combination. Some are comforting, some are unexpected, and all are made to be enjoyed together.

Explore a few favourites from our kitchen, then find the one you would like to bring to yours.

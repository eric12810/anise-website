The Anise philosophy

Three essentials. One great dish.

Chinese cuisine has evolved over thousands of years around three simple essences: 色, 香, 味 — visual, aroma and taste. At Anise, each one matters.

Vegan at Anise

A tradition of 2,000 years.

Chinese vegan cooking has a rich history in Buddhist and Taoist traditions. At Anise, we recreate these dishes with fresh ingredients and natural flavours, without MSG, food colourings or canned vegetables.
